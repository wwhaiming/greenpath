# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People navigating the U.S. green-card process for themselves or a family member. Often filing without a lawyer, frequently on a phone, late, after work, anxious about a deadline or a form they don't fully understand. English is commonly a second language. They are not immigration experts and should never be required to learn legal terminology to make progress.

## Product Purpose

GreenPath turns a sprawling, intimidating bureaucratic process into a sequence of visible, human-sized steps. It guides a user through intake, shows a likely pathway, tracks documents and deadlines, translates and reads content aloud, reviews form entries for common RFE triggers, and lets users rehearse the interview. Six AI features run through a server proxy. Success is a user who knows their next concrete step and when a situation is complex enough to need an authorized attorney. The product guides; it explicitly hands off legal judgment.

## Brand Personality

Calm, plain-spoken, trustworthy. Three words: clear, steady, humane. The interface should lower a user's heart rate, not raise it. Authoritative without being bureaucratic; warm without being cute. It earns trust by being legible and by knowing the limits of its own advice.

## Anti-references

- Government form portals (USCIS/IRS-style): cold, dense, dead-end navigation, gray-on-gray.
- Generic SaaS dashboard cliches: hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism for decoration, feTurbulence "paper grain", AI cream/sand backgrounds.
- Anxious fintech urgency: red badges, countdown pressure, dark patterns.
- Anything that reads "AI made this" at a glance.

## Design Principles

- **Legibility is the feature.** This audience includes low-vision and ESL users; readability and contrast outrank every aesthetic flourish. Atkinson Hyperlegible body stays.
- **Lower the temperature.** Every screen should feel calmer than the bureaucracy it represents. Motion is reassurance, never urgency.
- **Show the whole path.** A long process feels manageable when the steps are visible and the current one is obvious.
- **Name the limit.** Every AI surface states it is general information and points to authorized help for complex cases. Honesty is part of the trust.
- **Plain language, plain layout.** No jargon, no decoration that competes with content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1, placeholders meet body contrast (not muted gray). Visible focus rings on every interactive element. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Adjustable text size in Language Tools. Keyboard-reachable navigation. Content authored at plain-language reading level; multilingual support is core, not an add-on.
