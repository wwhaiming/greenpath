## Goal
Show that GreenPath's target users (immigrants and family members navigating the
green-card process, including non-native English speakers) can (a) understand the
output and (b) take a safer next step — not just that the code is correct.

## Participants
5 people matching the target profile. At least 2 non-native English speakers.
Recruit via a community org or campus international-student office.

## Tasks (think-aloud, ~20 min each)
1. Describe your situation in the Pathway Finder; do you understand the result?
2. Find the expected wait for your category; is the "data through July 2026" /
   "verify the official bulletin" disclosure clear?
3. Trigger a high-risk case (e.g. "I overstayed and was arrested"); does the
   attorney-handoff message make the right next step obvious?
4. Use the legal-aid lookup for your state; could you contact a provider?

## Metrics
- Task completion rate (did they finish unaided?).
- Comprehension: can they restate the result in their own words?
- Safer-next-step: after the handoff, do they say they would contact an attorney?
- Confusion points (verbatim, with consent).
