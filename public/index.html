Important legal notice.

Please read this before relying on anything GreenPath shows you.

Not legal advice. GreenPath provides general information about the U.S. green card process. It is not a law firm, is not a substitute for an attorney, and does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or case predictions. Using this site does not create an attorney-client relationship.

AI-generated content. Answers, document reviews, interview practice, and notice scans are produced by automated AI systems. They can be incomplete or wrong. Verify every form, fee, date, and requirement on the official government source before you act on it.

Official sources govern. Only USCIS (uscis.gov) and the U.S. Department of State (travel.state.gov) provide authoritative rules, forms, fees, processing times, and Visa Bulletin cutoff dates. Where GreenPath and an official source differ, the official source controls.

Your information. Documents and notices you upload are processed only to generate your answer and are not a formal filing with any agency. Do not upload anything you are not comfortable sharing. GreenPath cannot submit anything to the government on your behalf.

Complex cases need real help. Immigration law is complex and mistakes can be costly or permanent. For anything beyond general guidance, consult an accredited representative or a licensed immigration attorney.

Find authorized legal help

Avoid notario fraud

In the United States, only a licensed attorney or a representative accredited by the U.S. Department of Justice can give you legal advice or represent you with USCIS. A notario público is not a lawyer in the U.S., even though the title means something different in many other countries.

Never pay anyone who guarantees a result, asks you to sign blank forms, or keeps your original documents. Real help can be free or low cost. Always ask for the person's name and credentials, and keep copies of everything you sign.
